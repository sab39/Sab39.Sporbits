Sporbits

- UX
  + Camera
    - Design agreed in Sab39.Sabric/Docs/WIP/camera.md, which covers the Sporbits
      side too
    - Camera stays centered on PlayerPlanet
    - Starfield background with very slight parallax
    - Minimap, scaling to fit player, puck and a level-defined initial area
  - Mouse control - accelerate toward cursor while lmb held
    - Needs Camera.ToWorld, so it follows the camera work
- Gameplay
  - Objectives:
    + Goal
      + Circle with no gravity and maybe a repulsive force on nearby objects
      * Player touches it = lose, Puck touches it = win
    - Survival
      - Keeping puck inside a (shrinking?) circle
  - Player and/or puck planet takes damage rather than immediate destruction on collision
  - "Kick" mechanic
    - Hold to charge, release to cast
    - Applies an impulse to the puck directly away from player, proportional
      to charge and inverse square of distance
    - Maybe limited "fuel" for this and only some levels have fuel
  - Level ideas:
    + Steady/increasing stream of planets/asteroids flying in from the right
      - Works with either objective
    + Solar system - there's a fixed sun with high gravity that the player
      is initially orbiting
      - Goal could also be in orbit, or could be the sun itself
      * Other planets in the system serve as obstacles
    - Puck itself comes flying in from outside, player has to capture it
    - Puck starts out orbiting another planet, you have to steal it
    - Opposing player with their own goal actively trying to score against you
    - "Protect the innocent" mechanic
      - some other planets are inhabited, mustn't mess with their orbit too
        much etc.
