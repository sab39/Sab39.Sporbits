- Gameplay
  - Sun is a hazard for the puck
  - Tune the puck repulsion
    - Add damping so the puck can be captured into a close-but-not-too-close
      orbit of other planets without being completely unrecoverable.
  - Damage rather than instakill for player planet on contact
    - proportional to force of impact
    - tidal effects from proximity also damaging?
  - Alternate objective: survival
    - Keep the puck inside a (shrinking) ellipse
  - Varying shaped / moving hazard areas
  - "Kick" mechanic
    - Hold to charge, release to cast
    - Applies an impulse to the puck directly away from player, proportional
      to charge and inverse square of distance
    - Maybe limited "fuel" for this and only some levels have fuel
